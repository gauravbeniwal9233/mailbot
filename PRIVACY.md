# mailbot Privacy Policy
mailbot is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own
Gmail and Google Calendar to sort mail and manage events. Email content is sent
to Anthropic's Claude API solely to sort messages and draft replies. No data is
sold or shared with anyone else. All other data is processed and stored only on
the owner's own device.
